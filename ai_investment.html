Research Background

Efficiency of Investment from a Traditional Perspective

  1. Principal-agent problem: The Objective Function of the agent is not consistent with that of the principal, which leads to the problem of investment efficiency(Jensen and Meckling 1976; Jensen 1986).
  2. Information asymmetry: The principal cannot obtain the complete information of the agent, which leads to the problem of investment efficiency(Myers and Majluf 1984).

3. Uncertainty The principal cannot obtain the complete information of the agent, which leads to the problem of investment efficiency(Julio and Yook 2012).

The effect of Artificial Intelligence

  1. Modify the weight of public information, improve the accuracy of management’s judgment(Bianchi et al. 2022)
  2. Enhance the productivity of employees(Brynjolfsson et al. 2025)
  3. Promote total factor productivity(Acemoglu 2025)
  4. Improve corporate performance and growth rate(Mishra et al. 2022; Babina et al. 2024)
  5. Reduce the risk premium of investment(Vespignani and Smyth 2024)
